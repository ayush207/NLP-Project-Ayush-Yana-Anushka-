How it works

Three simple steps, from resume to match score.

1

Read both texts

The AI takes a job ad and a resume, and cleans the text so only meaningful words are left.

2

Compare and score

Two methods compare them: one counts shared words, the other understands meaning. Result: a score out of 100.

3

Show the skills

The report lists which required skills were found and which are missing, so a recruiter can double check.

What we found

Tested on 15 made-up candidate profiles and 302 real resume–job pairs.

15 of 15short versions scored higher than the wordy version of the same person
−91%drop in word-matching score for one profile after rewording
12%of skills still found once they were written in long form

Meaning: scores can help a recruiter, but should never decide alone.
